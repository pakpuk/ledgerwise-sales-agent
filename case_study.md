# Case study: AI sales platform for Ledgerwise (Unscale)

> Source brief pasted by the candidate. Do not edit.

## Why this case
Unscale builds AI systems that bring clients new customers: finding the right companies, reaching out to them, and turning replies into booked calls.
Here, the candidate plays the AI engineer on one of those projects. The client, Ledgerwise (fictional, but typical), wants an AI platform that finds leads, writes to them, answers their replies and books calls, with their sales team in control.

Build that platform, deploy it, and show how it was built. About 2 days of focused work. 72 hours to send it (timer starts when access to the document is requested).

Use any language, framework and tools. Use AI as much as you want; what is evaluated is how well it is directed. Claude Code or Codex recommended. Free tools and free tiers are fine. The candidate must know every detail of what was built; any part can be asked about.

## The client
Ledgerwise is a US bookkeeping firm. It does monthly bookkeeping for e-commerce brands. Today its sales team finds leads by hand, writes every first email by hand, and answers every reply by hand. They want a platform that does most of this while a human stays in control.

Who they sell to:
- US e-commerce brands that sell online (Shopify, Amazon, their own site).
- Roughly $1M to $10M yearly revenue, 5 to 50 employees.
- The person to contact is the founder, the COO or whoever runs finance.

Good reasons to reach out now:
- They are hiring for finance, accounting or operations.
- They recently raised money.
- They are growing into new channels: wholesale, Amazon, retail stores, new countries.

## Rules Ledgerwise gave
- Never quote a price. Pricing is discussed on a call.
- Never claim anything about a company that is not true. Every fact in an email must come from a real source.
- If someone says stop, unsubscribe or "not interested", stop and never contact them again.
- If someone asks whether they are talking to a bot, answer honestly.
- Anything the AI is not sure about goes to a human.

## The job
An AI agent that does five things:
1. **Research.** Find at least 30 real companies that fit the customer profile, and collect what is needed about each one.
2. **Qualify.** Decide which companies fit and how well. Show why, with the source for each reason (a link, a quote).
3. **Write.** Draft a short, personal first email for each qualified company. Every fact in it must come from the research.
4. **Reply.** When a prospect replies, the agent answers. It answers questions, handles objections, follows the rules above, and knows when to hand the thread to a human.
5. **Book calls.** When a prospect wants to talk, the agent offers real free slots and books the call in a calendar.

And a platform the Ledgerwise sales team uses every day. They are not technical. They must be able to:
- See the companies, their score and the reasons behind it.
- Review each email before it goes out: approve, edit or reject it.
- Read every conversation and take over any thread.
- See the calls the agent booked.

How it looks and how easy it is to use matters as much as the agent itself.
